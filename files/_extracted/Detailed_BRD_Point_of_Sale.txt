Detailed BRD: Point of Sale
Overview
The Simplified Point of Sale (POS) module is the main interface for hardware store staff. Since staff have
limited technical skills, the module is distraction-free, intuitive, and free of complex features. Its main
functions let staff complete transactions and trigger background processes (inventory deduction and
transaction logging) without needing advanced computer knowledge.
Scope
Browsing or searching for available hardware items
Adding items and adjusting quantities in a virtual cart
Real-time calculation of subtotals and grand totals
Finalizing a checkout transaction
Automated deduction of sold items from the central inventory
Automated logging of the transaction (including timestamp and the active staffʼs username)
Out-of-Scope
Direct hardware integration with physical credit card terminals (payments are assumed cash or
handled on a separate manual terminal)
Barcode scanner or receipt printer integration
Complex returns, refunds, or exchange processing
Applying discounts or coupons

Non-Functional Requirements
Constraints
The system shall clear
the cart and reset the
POS interface to a blank
state immediately after a
sale is finalized.
System Should Prepares the terminal for
the next customer in line
without manual clearing.
Usability The POS interface shall utilize large
typography, high-contrast colors,
and a maximum of 4 interactive
elements (Search, Add, Quantity
Adjust, Checkout) on the main
screen.
Performance The total price calculation must
update in under 0.5 seconds
whenever an item quantity is
adjusted.
Reliability The "Complete Sale" button shall
disable immediately after one click
to prevent accidental double-billing
or double-inventory deduction.
Usability The interface must be fully operable
on standard resolution desktop
monitors without requiring vertical
or horizontal scrolling for the main
cart view.
Category Requirement

The system requires an active local network or internet connection to communicate with the central
database; there is no offline mode.
Prices are strictly governed by the Admin; Staff cannot modify the unit price of an item during
checkout.
Limitations
Because there is no barcode scanner in this phase, search speed relies entirely on the staffʼs typing
speed and the search algorithm's efficiency.
Assumptions
All product details (name, price, initial stock) have been pre-configured in the database by the Admin
prior to the Staff initiating a sale.
Staff members will visually verify that the physical stock at the counter matches before clicking
"Complete Sale."

Detailed BRD: Demand Forecasting & Order Suggestions
Overview
The Demand Forecasting & Order Suggestions module is the intelligent core of SMART-STOCK,
designed to eliminate the guesswork from hardware store inventory management. By automatically
analyzing historical sales data processed through the POS, this module predicts future product demand.
It alerts the Admin before items run out and provides mathematically backed recommendations on
exactly what to restock and in what quantities, ensuring capital is not tied up in slow-moving stock while
preventing stockouts of highly requested products.
Scope
Continuous background analysis of daily sales velocity per item
Calculation of reorder points based on historical demand
Generation of a "Suggested Orders" dashboard for the Admin
Displaying recommended restock quantities to return inventory to optimal levels
Ability for the Admin to dismiss a suggestion or mark it as "Ordered"
Exporting the suggested order list (e.g., to CSV/PDF) to facilitate physical purchasing
Out-of-Scope
Direct integration with external hardware supplier APIs
Automated creation and emailing of Purchase Orders (POs) to vendors
Tracking supplier lead times or delivery transit times dynamically
Predicting seasonal spikes based on external factors (e.g., weather data or market trends)

Non-Functional Requirements
Constraints
The accuracy of the forecast is strictly bound by the accuracy of the POS data; if staff fail to ring up
items correctly, the suggestions will be skewed.
Forecasts rely purely on linear historical data and cannot natively predict sudden, unprecedented
spikes in demand.
Limitations
Because the system does not track supplier lead times, the 7-day safety threshold is a static
assumption that may not be sufficient for hard-to-source materials.
Performance Forecasting calculations shall run
asynchronously (e.g., via a
scheduled nightly job) to prevent UI
lag during regular business hours.
Usability The dashboard must clearly
differentiate critically low items
(e.g., under 3 days of supply) using
distinct visual indicators (like red
text/icons).
Reliability If an item has less than 7 days of
sales history, the system shall
bypass the forecasting algorithm
and rely on a static minimum
threshold (e.g., 10 units).
Security Any attempt to access the
suggestion export endpoint without
a valid Admin session token shall
result in a 403 Forbidden response.
Category Requirement

Assumptions
The store owner reviews the dashboard regularly (e.g., weekly) to place orders before the 7-day
safety stock depletes.
The business prefers to maintain approximately one month (30 days) of stock on hand for standard
items to balance storage space and availability.

Business rules
Daily Velocity = Total Units Sold / 30 days.
Reorder Point (Threshold) = Daily Velocity * 7 days.
Suggested Order Quantity = (Daily Velocity * 30 days) - Current Stock.
If Current Stock <= Reorder Point ➔  Add to Suggestions List.

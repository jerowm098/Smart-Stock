Detailed BRD: Account Management
Overview
SMART-STOCK ensures that the hardware store's inventory and financial data remain secure while
tracking employee accountability. The Accounts and Access module controls who can enter the system,
assigns specific roles (Admin vs. Staff), restricts what each role can see, and ensures every finalized
transaction is explicitly tied to the cashier who handled it.
Scope
Creation of user accounts by an Administrator
Role assignment (Admin or Staff)
Signing in and signing out
Session duration and tracking the active user for sales logs
Changing/resetting passwords manually by Admin
Restricting page access based on user role
Out-of-Scope
Self-service account registration (staff cannot create their own accounts)
Sign in with Google / Apple / Facebook
Two-factor authentication (2FA)
Email-based automated password recovery (Admin handles resets)
Customer/buyer accounts

Category Requirement

Constraints
No external email service integration; all password resets must be done manually by the Admin within
the system.
Sessions are stored in the single relational database.
Limitations
Because accounts cannot be fully deleted (to preserve sales history), the database will retain inactive
user records indefinitely.
Assumptions
One person, one account. Staff members do not share accounts with each other during shift changes.
Users have cookies enabled on the store's terminal browser.
The system operates on a trusted local network or secure terminal within the hardware store.

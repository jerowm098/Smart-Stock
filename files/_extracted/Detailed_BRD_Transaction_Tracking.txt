Detailed BRD: Transaction Tracking
Overview
The Transaction Tracking module serves as the primary auditing and accountability tool for the
hardware store owner. Every time a sale is finalized in the POS module, a permanent record is generated
here. This allows the Admin to review daily sales performance, investigate discrepancies, and verify
exactly which staff member processed specific transactions.
Scope
Automated logging of all finalized POS sales
Displaying a chronological master list of historical transactions
Viewing the itemized breakdown of a specific transaction (items sold, quantities, and unit prices)
Filtering transaction history by date range and by specific cashier (Staff username)
Calculating the total sales revenue for a filtered view
Out-of-Scope
Processing returns, voids, or refunds directly through the transaction log
Printing physical paper receipts from historical records
Direct API integration with third-party accounting software
Tracking customer names or buyer profiles associated with the transaction

Category Requirement

Constraints
Because records are strictly immutable, cashiers who make a mistake during checkout cannot "void"
the transaction in the system afterward; the Admin must issue a manual inventory adjustment to
correct the stock.
Limitations
The system relies entirely on the server's internal clock for timestamps; if the server time drifts,
transaction logs will reflect the inaccurate time.
Assumptions
Hardware store staff do not need to review their own past transactions once the current customer is
cleared.
Revenue tracking is based purely on the gross total of items sold; tax calculations and net profit
margins (deducting wholesale cost) are handled externally by the owner.

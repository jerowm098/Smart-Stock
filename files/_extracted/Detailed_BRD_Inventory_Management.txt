Detailed BRD: Inventory Management
Overview
The Inventory Monitoring module is the central command center for the hardware store owner (Admin).
It provides real-time visibility into the current stock levels of all products, tools, and materials. Beyond
simple tracking, this module actively works to prevent stockouts by analyzing sales velocity to generate
automated restocking suggestions. It also provides the necessary tools to manage the product catalog,
update pricing, and make manual adjustments for lost or damaged goods.
Scope
Viewing a comprehensive master list of all hardware items and current stock levels
Adding new products to the system catalog (defining name, ID, unit price, and initial stock)
Editing existing product details or deactivating discontinued items
Manual stock adjustments (adding restocked deliveries or deducting damaged/lost items)
Automated low-stock visual indicators
Demand forecasting and automated order suggestions based on recent sales trends
Out-of-Scope
Automated Purchase Order (PO) generation sent directly to external suppliers
Multi-warehouse or multi-branch inventory syncing
Supplier database management or price comparison tools
Barcode generation and printing

Category Requirement

Constraints
The Demand Forecasting formula relies strictly on historical sales data recorded within SMART-
STOCK; it cannot pull external market trends.
No automatic supplier purchasing; the Admin must physically place the orders based on the system's
suggestions.
Limitations
The accuracy of the inventory entirely depends on the staffʼs diligence in ringing up items correctly at
the POS and the Admin consistently logging new deliveries.
Assumptions
The store owner (Admin) will perform periodic physical inventory counts to ensure the physical stock
matches the system's digital records.
The business operates on a standard First-In-First-Out (FIFO) retail model, and individual product
expiration dates (like paint) do not need to be rigorously tracked by the system.
